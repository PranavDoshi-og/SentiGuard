    How SentiGuard Detects Zero-Day Phishing

    Unlike static blocklists that lag behind newly registered malicious domains, SentiGuard extracts 25 deep lexical, transport, and structural features from raw URLs and evaluates them through an ensemble Random Forest model trained on verified threat feeds.

    Lexical & Entropy Analysis

    Evaluates domain length, character entropy, hyphen count, and subdomain nesting depth to identify algorithmically generated domains (DGA) and brand impersonation.

    Obfuscation Heuristics

    Uncovers evasion tricks such as hex-encoded IP addresses, userinfo credential redirect tokens (@), double slashes in paths, and high-risk free TLDs.

    Real-Time Mobile QR Engine

    Extracts encoded QR payloads directly in the browser via WebRTC camera streams or uploaded raster assets, protecting mobile users from malicious redirects before browser resolution.
